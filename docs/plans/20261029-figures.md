# Figures: a photo sticker that acts as a puppet on the set

Rev 2 (2026-10-29): revised after the auto plan review.
- **Coverage.** Coverage is the sticker's existing `on` set plus `open: true`, and `mergeFrame`
  appends new frames to open figures. There is no `from`/`to`.
- **Base pose.** Figures keep a base `x/y/size/rot` as the fallback pose.
- **Placement on the capture screen.** A figure placed there travels in `Outstanding` and is created
  at settle.
- **Pruning.** Keyframes are pruned in `pruneEdits` with the pre-removal order, and a removed keyframe
  is carried forward to the next entry.
- **Undo.** History is cleared when a capture writes figure poses.
- **Parsing.** `Outstanding` is parsed leniently, and dropped keyframes count for the corrupt copy.
- **One resolver.** A single `stickersAt` resolver serves every reader of `on` and of the pose.
- **Live overlay.**
  - It keeps its own pose state across the shutter.
  - It shows only open figures.
  - It has no pinch.
  - It maps to the contained 4:3 rect.
  - Its tools sit in a fixed slot.
- **Task order.** The capture plumbing is split out and moved earlier.
- **Dropped.** The FOV correction and the extra soft-edge pass are gone.

## Overview
A sticker made in the new style **"Figura"** becomes a **figure**: a character that lives on the set
from the frame it was placed on to the end of the movie, frames shot later included, and that the kid
moves frame by frame like a real stop-motion puppet.

- **While shooting.** The figure is drawn over the live camera view. The kid drags it to its next spot
  (move, resize/rotate with the corner handle, mirror) and presses the shutter. The pose is stored for
  the new frame. The pose of the previous frame shows as a faint ghost. If she does not touch it, it
  stays where it was.
- **Later, in editing.** She selects a frame on the strip and corrects the figure's pose on that frame
  only. "Poprzednia poza" removes the frame's own pose.
- **Between poses.** No in-betweening: a frame without its own pose shows the last pose before it
  ("step").
- **No shadow, no white outline** (owner, 2026-10-29).
- The same renderer draws figures on the stage, over the live view and in the export.

**Owner decisions (2026-10-29):**
- move while shooting **and** correct later;
- no shadow for now;
- step between poses;
- a figure is a sticker made in the "Figura" style. In "Moje" figures are marked and placed as figures.

**Not in this plan:**
- smooth in-betweening;
- a shadow setting;
- a figure behind real objects (needs set segmentation);
- pinch gestures;
- layer order beyond "last placed on top";
- moving figures on the phone (remote-lite).

**Protocol:** PROTOCOL v1 and the camera app are unchanged. Tablet-only.

## Context (from discovery, checked by the review)
- **Entries and frames.**
  - The first entry of a frame has `id == frameId`.
  - `timeline()` = cut entries, then unreferenced frames in `seq` order.
  - Hidden and off entries stay in `timeline()`.
  - `deleteEntry` removes duplicate entries directly. For a frame's last entry it returns
    `hideFrameId`: the frame is hidden (5 s Undo), then `expireFrames` → `pruneEdits`
    (`remote/lib/projects/reducers.ts`) removes the frame's entries and prunes sticker `on` ids.
  - `pruneEdits` also runs from `restoreSnapshot` and the sweep.
- **Stickers.**
  - `Sticker {id, emoji, x, y, size, rot, on}`.
  - `parseStickers` keeps unknown keys, so new fields survive a write by an older build.
  - `droppedEditFields` compares array lengths only.
  - `pruneEdits` keeps a sticker with `on: []`.
  - Readers of `on` and of the pose:
    - `drawStickers`, `stickersOn`, `pickSticker`, `withLivePose`;
    - `StickerGesture` (starts from `s.x`, commits via `updateSticker`);
    - `StickerLayer` (`stickerBox(sel)`);
    - PlayView `paintStickers`;
    - `stripTiles` (`marked`);
    - `StickerController.ends`;
    - the export preload and draw.
  - Gestures: single-pointer drag plus one corner handle for scale+rotate. There is no pinch.
- **History.** `EditHistory` is an app-wide stack of `{cut, stickers}` snapshots per project
  (AppShell `editors`). `restoreSnapshot` replaces `stickers` wholesale, and there is no redo.
- **Capture.**
  - `press(projectId)` refuses while any outstanding entry exists for the camera (limit 1), and the
    shutter is disabled while outstanding.
  - `beginCapture` persists `Outstanding {reqId, cameraId, sentAt}`. `settleFrame` removes it and calls
    `mergeFrame` in one mutation (merge status inserted/exists/tombstoned/wrong_*).
  - Reconcile (`listFrames`) can merge a frame whose outstanding entry was given up.
  - `parseProject` rebuilds outstanding entries strictly (unknown keys stripped, bad shape →
    `ModelError`).
- **Capture screen.**
  - `CaptureView` has no StickerController, history or picker (these live in AppShell for PlayView).
  - `.preview-stage` is not 4:3 on 1280×754: the canvas letterboxes (`objectFit: contain`).
  - The shutter sits centred in `.cap-bar` (column 2), and `globals.css` warns against moving it.
  - Preview and stills share one ViewPort/transform: Stage 1 "preview and onion skin line up in the
    grid test".
- **Sticker maker.**

  - `StyleCache` is keyed by the style string.
  - `library.parseIndex` rebuilds items, so an older build drops unknown item fields.

## Solution Overview
1. **A figure is a sticker with `kind: "figure"`.** It keeps all sticker fields, so ids, sprites,
   copy-on-place, `setRange`, `duplicateEntry` and `pruneEdits` work unchanged:
   - **Coverage** is `on`, as for stickers.
   - **`open: true`** means "until the end": `mergeFrame` appends each newly merged frame (capture settle
     **and** reconcile) to the `on` of every open figure.
   - **The base `x/y/size/rot`** is the pose on the first covered entry and the fallback.
   - **`poses: {entryId: FigurePose}`** holds the keyframes.
2. **Pose resolution (step).** For a covered entry, the pose is the keyframe on the nearest covered entry
   at or before it in timeline order, else the base pose. Export computes the poses once per timeline
   (O(n)), not per frame.
3. **One resolver**, `stickersAt(project, entryId): Sticker[]`. It returns the stickers and covering
   figures on the entry, with the resolved pose merged into x/y/size/rot (+`flip`). The renderer, hit
   test, layer, gestures, PlayView, strip marks and export go through it. Pose writes for a figure go to
   `setFigurePose(entry)` instead of `updateSticker`.
4. **Capture plumbing.**
   - **At the shutter**, the overlay's current poses of all open figures (touched or not) go into the
     `Outstanding` entry as `figurePoses: {stickerId: FigurePose}`. Figures placed on the capture screen
     since the last shot go in as `newFigures: [{id, emoji, pose}]`. Their picture is copied into the
     movie at placement.
   - **At `settleFrame`**, in the merge mutation:
     - `newFigures` become figures with `on: [frameId]`, `open: true` and base pose = their pose;
     - `figurePoses` become `poses[frameId]` for figures that still exist and cover the frame.
     - This happens only for merge status `inserted`.
   - **A dropped capture** drops both.
   - **A frame recovered by reconcile** (outstanding given up) joins open figures through `mergeFrame`
     without its own pose, so it shows the previous pose by step (accepted, documented).
5. **History.** A settle that writes `poses` or `newFigures` clears the project's EditHistory, so
   Cofnij can never restore a snapshot that lacks captured keyframes.
6. **Pruning and edits** (`pruneEdits` gets the pre-removal timeline from `expireFrames`,
   `deleteEntry` and `restoreSnapshot`):
   - **A removed entry** leaves `on` (as today) and loses its keyframe. If the next covered entry (old
     order) has no keyframe, it inherits the removed one, so the following frames keep their look.
   - **When the first covered entry is removed**, the next covered entry takes over the base pose (via
     the same carry-forward).
   - **A figure is removed** only when `on` is empty and it is not `open`.
   - **Hidden frames keep their keyframes** until expiry.
   - **`setFigurePose(entry)`** also pins the next covered entry to its old resolved pose when that
     entry has no keyframe, so "this frame only" holds.
   - **`duplicateEntry`** copies the keyframe to the copy (the copy is already added to `on`).
   - **`moveEntry`** keeps keyframes on their entries, and step resolution follows the new order.
   - **Unknown keyframe ids** are kept by parse and dropped by `pruneEdits`, like `on`.
7. **Live overlay** (capture screen):
   - **Own state.** The overlay holds the live poses. They are initialised on entering the screen from
     the pose at the last covered entry. They are kept across the shutter, so the kid can already move
     for the next frame. They are reset only by "Cofnij pozę", by entering the screen, or by an edit
     that changes the last entry's pose.
   - **Ghost.** While an outstanding entry exists, it is drawn from `outstanding.figurePoses`;
     otherwise from the last entry's pose.
   - **Only open figures** are shown.
   - **Mapping** goes to the contained 4:3 rect of the preview canvas, not to the stage box.
   - **Gestures:** drag + the corner handle (scale+rotate) as in StickerLayer, plus a mirror button.
   - **Tools** ("+ Figura", "Odbij", "Cofnij pozę") sit in a fixed-height slot or next to the selected
     figure inside the preview. The shutter does not move.
8. **Style "Figura":** a `figura` branch in `makeSticker` = auto-enhance (when on) + trim + `padSquare`,
   with no outline and no shadow. The library index item gets an optional `kind: "figure"`, and "Moje"
   shows a badge.

## Technical Details
- **`model.ts`:**
  - `FigurePose = {x, y, size, rot, flip?: true}`;
  - `Sticker.kind?: 'figure'`, `Sticker.open?: true`, `Sticker.flip?: true`,
    `Sticker.poses?: Record<string, FigurePose>`;
  - `Outstanding.figurePoses?: Record<string, FigurePose>` and
    `Outstanding.newFigures?: {id, emoji, pose}[]`.
- **Parse:**
  - Figure fields are lenient: bad keyframes are dropped, and a figure with no valid keyframes keeps
    its base pose.
  - `Outstanding` extras are lenient: bad items are dropped and never throw.
  - `droppedEditFields` also counts dropped keyframes and outstanding extras, which triggers the
    `.corrupt-<ts>` copy.
- **`figures.ts`:**
  - `resolvePoses(project, sticker)` → `Map<entryId, FigurePose>` (one pass over the timeline);
  - `stickersAt(project, entryId)`;
  - `addFigure(project, entryId, emoji, id, pose, open = true)`;
  - `setFigurePose` (pins the next entry);
  - `clearFigurePose` (refused on the first covered entry);
  - `setFigureFlip`;
  - `setFigureOpen`;
  - `applyCaptureFigures(project, frameId, outstanding)`.
- **`reducers.ts`:**
  - `mergeFrame` appends to open figures;
  - `pruneEdits(project, prevTimeline?)` prunes keyframes with carry-forward;
  - `beginCapture(..., {figurePoses, newFigures})`.
- **`capture.ts`:** `press(projectId, figures?: {poses, newFigures})`.
- **`AppShell.tsx`** builds a per-project capture-side controller (copy-on-place, picker filtered to
  figures) and clears history on settles with figure data. The settle hook goes through the store
  listener or the resolver result.
