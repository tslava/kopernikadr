Kopernikadr

Kopernikadr is a stop-motion movie studio for kids and their parents. A phone is the camera, a tablet is the studio: shoot frames, add stickers and drawings, and export an MP4 movie. Everything stays on your own devices.

Two apps

  • Kopernikadr (tablet) – the studio: projects, stickers, movie export.
  • Kopernikadr Camera (phone) – the camera; it sends photos to the tablet over your home Wi‑Fi.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
