Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-03

This policy covers two Android apps: Kopernikadr (tablet, pl.slava.kopernikadr.remote) and Kopernikadr Camera (phone, pl.slava.kopernikadr.camera). They are made by a private individual (the developer). Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

In short

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server.

Where your photos and movies go

  • The camera app takes photos (frames) and sends them over your home Wi-Fi to your own tablet. They do not leave your local network.
  • Projects, stickers and exported MP4 movies stay on your devices. A finished movie is saved to the tablet's gallery.

Photo stickers

To cut a toy out of a photo, the tablet app uses Google MediaPipe. It runs on the tablet and the images are not uploaded anywhere. The MediaPipe library contains a usage-statistics component. We have disabled its upload, so no such data is sent.

Permissions and why

  • Camera app: camera (to take the photos); network and Wi-Fi access (to talk to the tablet on your local network); Wi-Fi multicast (so the tablet can find the phone on the local network).
  • Tablet app: network access (to connect to the camera on the local network); Wi-Fi multicast (to discover the camera). The tablet app has no camera permission of its own: for stickers it uses the system camera app or photo picker.

The "network" permission is only used inside your home network. We recommend using your own trusted Wi-Fi.

Android backup

The camera app opts out of Android backup. The tablet app does not, so if Google backup is turned on for the device, Android may back up its data (such as projects) to your Google account, according to your Google and device settings. We do not receive this data and cannot see it.

Children

The apps are designed for children together with their parents. We do not collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Deleting your data

All data is on your devices. Delete movies in the app to remove them, or uninstall the app to remove all its data. Movies already saved to the gallery can be deleted in the gallery. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
