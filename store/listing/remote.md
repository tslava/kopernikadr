## en-US

**Title** (11/30): `Kopernikadr`

**Short description** (75/80):

```
Make stop-motion movies at home: tablet as the studio, old phone as camera.
```

**Full description**:
```
Kopernikadr is a stop-motion studio for the home, made for kids and their parents. The tablet is your remote and editing table, and an old phone becomes the camera. You need both apps: this one on the tablet and "Kopernikadr Camera" on the phone. Both devices must be on the same home Wi-Fi. No internet connection and no accounts are needed.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Put the phone running "Kopernikadr Camera" in front of your set, on a stand or a stack of books.
2. Open Kopernikadr on the tablet. It finds the camera on your Wi-Fi by itself.
3. Arrange the scene, tap the shutter, nudge your figures a little and take the next photo. Frame by frame, a movie appears.
4. Watch it, polish it and save it to the tablet's gallery as an MP4 file.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Live view from the phone camera on the tablet, and a one-tap shutter.
- Onion skin: the previous frame shows through, so you can see how far to move your figure.
- Play the movie back, with adjustable speed (frames per second) and looping.
- Edit frames: delete with undo, copy, hold a frame longer or shorter, move, switch off, trim the start and end.
- Emoji stickers: hundreds of ready-made OpenMoji pictures. Place them, drag, resize and rotate them, and choose which frames they appear on.
- "Make a sticker": photograph your own toy or pick a photo from the gallery, tap the toy or draw around it with a finger, and the app cuts it out. Your sticker joins the sticker picker and goes into the movie like any emoji. The cut-out is done on the tablet, and the photo is not sent anywhere.
- Save the finished movie to the gallery as an MP4, ready to watch and share with the family.
- Keep many movies: create, rename and delete them (with undo).

FOR PARENTS
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics.
- Photos and movies stay on your own devices. The phone sends photos to the tablet over your home Wi-Fi only; the app does not upload them to any server.
- The app needs no internet beyond your home network. Android calls the permission "internet", and it is required for any network communication, including on a local network.
- A "For parents" panel (press and hold to open) holds the camera settings: exposure, white balance, focus, rotation, preview quality, frames per second, a grid, and diagnostic information.
- The app is available in English, Polish and Russian.

Emoji stickers come from the OpenMoji project (CC BY-SA 4.0). Toy cut-out uses MediaPipe and the MagicTouch model (Apache 2.0), running on the tablet.

Requirements: an Android 12 or newer tablet, plus a second device (an Android 12 or newer phone) running "Kopernikadr Camera", both on the same Wi-Fi network.
```
